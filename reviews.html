Frequently Asked Questions

What are the gym operating hours?

We operate Monday through Saturday from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM continuously. Sundays feature special morning conditioning slots.

Can beginners get a tour or trial workout?

Yes! Prospective members can visit our Mahabirtala floor, meet our trainers, assess the equipment, and discuss their health goals with our desk staff.

Is general floor trainer support included?

Yes, floor trainers are on duty at all peak and non-peak hours to assist with machine adjustments, spot heavy lifts, and ensure injury-free execution.

Do you provide nutrition and meal planning guidance?

Absolutely. Our coaches provide tailored macronutrient roadmaps, grocery recommendations, and hydration strategies that align with your workout regimen.
