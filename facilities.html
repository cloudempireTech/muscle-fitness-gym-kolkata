The Equipment Infrastructure

Explore our specialized strength stations, powerlifting rigs, pin-loaded cables, and cardio theatre built for relentless workout consistency.

Free Weights

Commercial Dumbbell Tier (Up to 50 KG)

Dual rows of heavy-duty polyurethane dumbbells ascending from 2.5 kg up to 50 kg in strict 2.5 kg increments, complemented by adjustable decline, flat, and 90° incline benches.

  • Commercial grade anti-wobble locking benches
  • Ergonomic contoured handles for secure grip
Powerlifting Zone

Olympic Power Cages & Deadlift Decks

Heavy-gauge steel power racks with laser-cut pin holes, safety spotter arms, competition j-cups, and reinforced oak-center deadlift platforms.

  • 20 kg standard Olympic barbells with rotating sleeves
  • Color-coded competition Olympic bumper plates
Machine Circuit

Dual Cable Crossovers & Pin Stacks

Multi-station cable towers featuring independent weight stacks, high/low pulleys, rope attachments, revolving straight bars, and seated row/lat pulldown combo stations.

  • Smooth aircraft-grade steel braided cables
  • Instant pin adjustment up to 100 kg resistance
Cardio Theatre

Commercial AC Treadmills & Rowers

High-end continuous duty 5.0 HP motor treadmills with 15% power incline, elliptical cross-trainers, air assault bikes, and magnetic resistance rowing machines.

  • Shock-absorbing orthopaedic running decks
  • Real-time telemetry: heart rate, pace & calories
Functional & HIIT

Functional Cross-Training & Calisthenics

High-density turf track with weighted push sled, heavy battle ropes, competition cast-iron kettlebells, plyometric jump boxes, and gymnastics pull-up rigs.

  • Unanchored agility and core stability equipment
  • Wall balls, slam balls & resistance bands
Member Comfort

Lockers, Showers & Hydration

Spacious changing suites, private pin-code lockers, chilled UV-filtered hydration stations, and a dedicated warm-up & mobility stretching lounge.

  • Climate-controlled locker room bays
  • Dedicated supplement & pre-workout desk
